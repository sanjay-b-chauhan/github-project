# The laws (Sanjay Chauhan portfolio, v12)

1. **Three colours.** Black is the space. Cream `#FFFDE2` is the voice. Red `#FF0101` is light: threads, beams, rims, the sun. Red never fills a box, except the one button you want pressed.
2. **Three typefaces, three jobs.** Telgra says the name, the numbers and the labels (caps, tracked wide). Instrument Serif, always upright, says the one sentence. Inter Tight carries the small print. Nothing else, no italics, no script.
3. **One section, one idea, one object.** Every full-bleed screen has one hero object and one line. If it needs two sentences it becomes two scroll steps.
4. **Light is the layout.** Every scene starts pitch black. Things exist only where light lands. No flat fill light, no decorative gradients.
5. **Real materials, no toys.** Chrome, brass, stone, wood, concrete, glass, neon, particles. No confetti, no cartoon props, no emoji.
6. **The camera tells the story.** We go from the top to the ground. Every change is the camera moving down. One scroll is one step, and it snaps.
7. **Slow in, slow out.** Moves take 1 to 2.5 seconds on an ease in and out. Text arrives blur to sharp or letter by letter. Nothing bounces.
8. **One thread through everything.** A single red line (the sutradhar's cord) holds the plumb bob, hangs the work and ties the three generations.
9. **Sound is quiet.** A low drone and soft plucks after the first click, a tick on hover, one button to mute.
10. **Few words, all true.** Sentence case, no dashes, no hype. Real clients, real work, real team names. No invented numbers.
11. **The phone keeps the idea.** Same object, reframed for portrait. Text sits above the object. Nothing shrinks into unreadable.
