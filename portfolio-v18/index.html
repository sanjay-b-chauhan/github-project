        02 / About

        Hello, I’m Sanjay.

        Born and raised in Mumbai, from a Suthar family of builders whose roots are in Rajasthan. My grandfather built with wood. My father builds with concrete. I build with pixels.

        I studied electronics engineering and taught myself design on the side. Graphic designer, then Head of Design at Intent, now Senior Product Designer at ZERO in Dubai. Product, brand, motion, 3D and code. I would rather build the thing than explain it. It saves a lot of meetings.
