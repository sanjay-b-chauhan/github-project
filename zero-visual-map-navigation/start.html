Zero World · visual map navigation · Aug 20

The map already knows
the whole journey.

Sixteen scenarios, sixteen buildings, and until now the screen showed you one of them. The rail stays exactly where it is — it is a good index. What changes is the world behind it: the journey is drawn on the city as a road, solid where you have been and dotted where you are going, with every stop marked on the building it belongs to.

Look for “Whole journey” in the bottom-left corner — it pulls the camera back until all sixteen stops are on screen at once. Click any of them to fly there. Desktop viewing.

 00 · reference

The screen as it ships today

Every token and injection off, leaving the deployed base untouched.
Built as an injection over the deployed nav lab — the base bundle is byte-identical underneath. The road reads its order and its status from the journey fixture, its coordinates from the map’s own placement table and its logos from the rail’s own chips, and a stop’s click is forwarded to that scenario’s rail chip, so nothing here can disagree with the product. Every screen carries a panel at the top to switch between them in place.
