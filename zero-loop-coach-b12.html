Zero · Navid, beside your work · b12 · 17 Oct 2026Open the journey

He waits until you look. Then he has the room.

Navid is the face of Zero, on your side for the whole journey. He never steps into the work; your manager owns that. When he has something, his face blinks. You click it, the app slides aside, and he says it in his own voice, two or three lines at a time. Got it, and you are back where you were.

The moment, in four beats

1

He blinks

Grey in the pill when he has nothing. Lit and blinking, with "Got a sec?", when he has something. Ignore it and nothing breaks.

2

The app slides aside

The whole app moves left as one piece. The right 30% turns black and his face travels into it.

3

He says it

His voice, with the words in the middle of the panel, two or three lines at a time, lighting up as he speaks.

4

You answer

Two keys: Got it, or Why. Why gets one more line, then Okay, got it, and the app slides back.

Twice is a slip, the third time he steps in

In the kickoff, every number Jay gives is a note. Sanjay lets the first go by: a faint "not written" row in his notes. The second: Navid's face starts to blink. The third: Navid comes in without being asked, names the three numbers, and Sanjay writes them down while he watches. That is the one place he does not wait for a click.

Jump to any moment

The bar under the app has Navid's face with two arrows. Each arrow goes straight to his next or previous moment in the case, plays it, and stops there. Speed is beside it: 1x, 2x, 4x.

After a few moments, he offers "I have a question". Three questions he can answer now, from what Zero already knows, and a field for your own that says Soon. If people use it, it becomes the chat.
