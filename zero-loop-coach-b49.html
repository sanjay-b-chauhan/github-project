Fourteen times Navid steps in. Each time, one question.

One scenario, the inbound lead router at HubSpot. Sanjay skips notes, guesses, and sends things he should not. Navid catches each one at the moment it happens. He says what he saw, asks one question that makes Sanjay think, and gives one push. Ask why and he explains. Sanjay fixes it himself. The fixes keep 900 skill points. He finishes at Meeting Expectations, 1,000 points, and ranks up to Rank 2.

Rank is earned, not given. Doing scenarios does not rank you up. Skill points do, and skill points come from doing the work well.

1,000 skill points per rank. A Meeting scenario pays 1,000, Exceeding 2,000, Exceptional 3,000.

Every intervention, the same eight beats

Learn it once and every intervention reads the same way, for the learner and for the build.

1

He notices

Zero logs the event: a skipped note, a message to the manager, minutes with no progress, a grade.

2

He comes in

Nothing waits in a pill. When he has something, he comes in on his own, and leaves when he is done.

3

The app shifts

The whole app slides left at full size with a rounded edge, and he takes the space it leaves.

4

He says it

Two or three lines at a time, in his voice. A ring around his face counts down to "back to work".

5

The stake

What it is worth, in skill points, before anything is asked of you: "+200 skill points, if you rewrite it".

6

Why, and good

Why? gives the reason and shows what good looks like, with its points. The ring adds the seconds.

7

The fix

He goes, the app comes back, and you make the fix. He does not come back to check it.

8

The points land

A coin flies from what you fixed into your rank. The rank bar fills up live.

The rules

When he comes in, and how.

Time up front

The pill says how long before you tap. The ring counts it down. Why adds its seconds, out loud.

Private slips: the third time

Notes and other private habits get covered twice by Zero. The third time is a habit, so Navid steps in.

Manager facing: at Send, the first time

Anything Jay will read is checked before he sees it. Caught there, it costs nothing.

Never mid performance

Nothing during a presentation or live call. Right after, before the feedback.

Every fix is points

Each intervention names its stake in skill points, and the points he keeps land in the rank pill.

Why always shows good

The reason is never alone. It comes with the example, so he knows exactly what to do.

He never checks again

He says it once, answers Why, and goes. No coming back with "That is it". The fix is yours.

No small praise

No "Wow, you are done". When you do it right he stays quiet. He calibrates the whole picture: notes, chats, the check, credits, submissions, the talk, jobs.

One fix per moment

One thing at a time, never two corrections back to back without a win between.

Colour

Zero’s own signal tokens, one job each.

Something to fix

Holds, slips, stuck, the ums. Zero’s --fix token.

A win

Points kept, the rank up, the recap. Zero’s --commit token.

Before you start

Sets the bar. Neutral.

Skill points only

The stake and the coin. Zero’s --reward token, the only colour allowed to glow.
